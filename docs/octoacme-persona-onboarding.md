# OctoAcme Persona Onboarding Guide

## Purpose
This guide provides quick-start instructions for new team members joining OctoAcme projects in different roles. Each persona section includes essential first steps, key artifacts to review, and suggested first-week activities.

---

## Developer

### Quick-Start (First 5 Steps)
1. **Set up development environment**: Clone the repository, install dependencies, and verify you can build and run tests locally
2. **Review technical documentation**: Read architecture docs, coding standards, and contribution guidelines
3. **Attend team standup**: Introduce yourself and understand current sprint goals
4. **Pick up a starter task**: Begin with a well-defined, small task to familiarize yourself with the codebase
5. **Submit your first PR**: Go through the code review process and understand team feedback practices

### Key Artifacts to Review
- Repository README and setup instructions
- Architecture and design documents
- Test coverage reports and CI/CD pipeline
- Coding standards and style guides

---

## Product Manager

### Quick-Start (First 5 Steps)
1. **Review product vision and roadmap**: Understand strategic goals, priorities, and success metrics
2. **Meet with stakeholders**: Schedule 1:1s with key stakeholders, sponsors, and team leads
3. **Study user research and data**: Review existing user research, analytics dashboards, and feedback channels
4. **Attend backlog refinement**: Observe how the team prioritizes and estimates work
5. **Draft your first feature spec**: Create acceptance criteria for an upcoming feature

### Key Artifacts to Review
- Product one-pagers and vision documents
- Roadmap and backlog in project management tool
- User research reports and personas
- Success metrics and KPI dashboards

---

## Project Manager

### Quick-Start (First 5 Steps)
1. **Review project plan and timeline**: Understand milestones, dependencies, and critical path
2. **Study the risk register**: Familiarize yourself with known risks and mitigation strategies
3. **Meet the core team**: Schedule introductions with Product Manager, Tech Lead, and key stakeholders
4. **Attend key ceremonies**: Observe standup, planning, and retrospective meetings
5. **Publish your first status update**: Draft and share a weekly status report

### Key Artifacts to Review
- Project charter and scope documents
- Risk register and decision log
- Communication plan and stakeholder map
- Previous status reports and meeting notes

---

## UX Designer

### Quick-Start (First 5 Steps)
1. **Review design system and brand guidelines**: Understand visual language, components, and accessibility standards
2. **Study existing user research**: Read research reports, personas, and journey maps
3. **Meet with Product Manager**: Align on user goals, success criteria, and upcoming priorities
4. **Audit current user experience**: Walk through existing flows and identify usability opportunities
5. **Attend backlog refinement**: Understand how design requirements are integrated into development planning

### Key Artifacts to Review (First Week)
- Design system documentation and component library
- User research repository (interviews, usability tests, surveys)
- Product roadmap and feature specifications
- Accessibility standards and compliance requirements
- Project one-pager for context on business goals

---

## Agile Coach

### Quick-Start (First 5 Steps)
1. **Observe team ceremonies**: Attend standup, planning, retrospective, and review meetings to assess team health
2. **Meet with team members**: Schedule 1:1s to understand team dynamics, challenges, and improvement desires
3. **Review process metrics**: Study velocity, cycle time, and other agile health indicators
4. **Identify initial improvement opportunities**: Note quick wins and systemic issues
5. **Facilitate your first retrospective**: Run a focused session to gather team feedback and create action items

### Key Artifacts to Review (First Week)
- Sprint/iteration reports and burn-down charts
- Previous retrospective notes and action items
- Team working agreements and ceremonies schedule
- Project risk register for process-related impediments
- Project one-pager to understand project goals and constraints

---

## Stakeholder Sponsor

### Quick-Start (First 5 Steps)
1. **Review business case and objectives**: Understand strategic rationale, expected ROI, and success criteria
2. **Meet with Product and Project Managers**: Establish communication cadence and escalation paths
3. **Identify organizational blockers**: Assess resource constraints, dependencies, and political challenges
4. **Review project status and risks**: Understand current state, timeline, and critical risks
5. **Communicate executive support**: Signal your sponsorship to the team and broader organization

### Key Artifacts to Review (First Week)
- Project charter and business case
- Product roadmap and success metrics
- Risk register (especially organizational and resource risks)
- Stakeholder map and communication plan
- Budget and resource allocation documents
- Project one-pager for quick context

---

## General Tips for All Personas
- **Ask questions early**: No question is too basic during onboarding
- **Build relationships**: Schedule coffee chats with cross-functional partners
- **Use the handoff checklist**: Reference the [Role Handoff Checklist](octoacme-role-handoff-checklist.md) for phase-specific responsibilities
- **Review related process docs**: Explore other OctoAcme documentation in the docs/ folder
- **Contribute to documentation**: Update docs when you find gaps or outdated information
